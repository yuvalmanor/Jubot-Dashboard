# Illiquid assets are held at cost and never re-valued

Real-estate projects are more than half the household's non-pension capital, and a
dashboard that never marks them to market looks like an omission. It is deliberate.

No current valuation exists for any of them and none will be sought: CGM 1 was valued when
it was bought and will not be re-assessed, CGM 2 has not been purchased yet, and Meteor
issues reports the household does not read. The only alternative to cost is a number
somebody invents, and an invented valuation on 52% of the portfolio is worse than an
honest cost basis — it would silently drive allocation percentages, מט"ח exposure and every
funding decision that keys off them.

So a Snapshot records each Account's value together with its Value Basis (שווי שוק / עלות /
הערכה), and the totals state how much is held at cost rather than measured. Where a
forward-looking number is genuinely wanted, the לוח תכנון supplies it as an explicit
assumption in a Scenario, or from recorded Deal Terms — never as a fact in מיפוי.

## Consequences

סך הון cannot answer "are we getting richer" for the real-estate half of the portfolio. It
answers "how much have we deployed". Any screen implying growth must either exclude
cost-held assets or show the appreciation assumption it applied.

A project's value does not change as its expense ledger is spent down — the money converts
from cash into property inside the same pot, and the pot's cost is unchanged.
