# Illiquid assets carry their latest stated valuation

Supersedes [0003](0003-illiquid-assets-are-held-at-cost.md), whose factual premise turned
out to be wrong.

0003 held every real-estate project at cost and refused re-valuation, and its whole
argument rested on one sentence: *"No current valuation exists for any of them and none
will be sought."* That is not the case.

The CGM projects are single-family homes held in fact, and SFH holdings of this kind are
re-appraised every few years. Meteor's sponsor reports performance — 20% growth over the
last year, stated by them. These are not numbers the household would be inventing. They are
external, dated and attributable, which is exactly the thing 0003 said did not exist and
which its reasoning depended on not existing.

So a project may be worth more than it cost, and the dashboard may say so.

## What keeps this honest

The distinction 0003 was protecting is real, and it does not need cost to survive — Value
Basis already carries it. A re-appraisal is שווי שוק. A sponsor's own statement of growth
is הערכה: it is somebody's judgement, and a judgement by the party being measured. עלות
remains what a project reads at when nothing has been stated about it, which will be the
usual case between appraisals.

A valuation is therefore a **recorded event, not a running number**: a figure, the date it
was stated, and the document or party it came from. A project's value does not drift
upward between statements, and nothing extrapolates an annual rate forward. What the
sponsor said last is what the project reads, until they say something else.

Cost stays visible beside it. The question "what have we put in" is a different question
from "what is it said to be worth", and the first one is the one that cannot be argued
with.

## Consequences

סך הון can now answer "are we getting richer" for the real-estate half of the portfolio,
with the caveat the basis carries: for some of it the answer is a professional appraisal
and for some of it the sponsor's own word.

Allocation percentages, מט"ח exposure and the funding decisions keyed off them now move
when a valuation is recorded. 0003 feared exactly this and was right to, for invented
numbers; the safeguard is that a valuation cannot be typed without a source, so the
provenance of any movement is on the screen that moved.

The paragraph in 0003 about a project's value not falling as its expense ledger is spent
down still holds and is unaffected: converting cash into property is not losing money,
whatever basis the property is then carried at.

A project with no recorded valuation is unchanged in every respect.
